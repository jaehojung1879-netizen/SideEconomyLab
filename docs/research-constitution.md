# Research Constitution v1

## 1. Decision
Select and validate a side business that can produce independent cash flow without materially impairing the owner's full-time employment or family routine.

## 2. Capital philosophy
- Seed capital is allowed.
- Preferred first-pilot capital: KRW 3m–7m.
- Normal first-pilot ceiling: about KRW 10m.
- Larger pilots require unusually strong evidence and a separate decision memo.
- Expansion should preferentially be financed from SideEconomy cash flow rather than repeated salary injections.

## 3. Labor philosophy
Allowed:
- scheduled evening work;
- weekend batch work;
- periodic site visits;
- review / approval of AI-generated operational actions.

Penalized heavily:
- unpredictable weekday calls;
- same-day emergency site attendance;
- daily packing / delivery;
- customer count scaling linearly with owner hours;
- businesses that require the owner to substitute for absent staff.

## 4. Business-model philosophy
The project is not limited to installation businesses. Eligible models include physical assets, rental, space, commerce, route businesses, B2B recurring transactions, service operations, production and physical-digital hybrids.

## 5. AI philosophy
AI is an operating lever, not a reason to choose a business.

Useful AI roles may include:
- demand / location screening
- forecasting
- inventory
- pricing
- scheduling
- exception detection
- customer-service triage
- route planning
- maintenance prediction
- weekly action prioritization

An opportunity does not receive a high score merely because AI can be attached to it.

## 6. Reversibility
Prefer:
- movable assets;
- resaleable equipment;
- short trial contracts;
- revenue-share hosting;
- low fixed rent;
- outsourced maintenance;
- experiments that can be killed cheaply.

## 7. Evidence discipline
- Sourced fact != estimate != assumption.
- Use ranges rather than false precision.
- Screen broadly before deep-diving.
- Use public data to reject weak opportunities, not to prove success.
- Real field behavior outranks desk research when they conflict.

## 8. Pilot rule
No pilot without a written preregistration containing:
- thesis
- target user / demand
- acquisition / location path
- full capex
- fixed and variable cost model
- downside / base / upside utilization
- owner-hours estimate
- owner-interruption estimate
- resale / exit value
- PASS / MOVE / FAIL rules

## 9. Current exclusion
Businesses materially dependent on employer-confidential information, internal customer relationships, or regulated financial advisory activity are outside initial scope.

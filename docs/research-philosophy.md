# Research Philosophy

This is an entrepreneurial discovery program, not a confirmatory backtest.

The goal is not to preserve outcome blindness. The goal is to learn quickly while avoiding self-deception.

## What we borrow from rigorous research
- explicit hypotheses
- common comparison metrics
- recorded assumptions
- source provenance
- preregistered pilot success / failure rules
- reproducible calculations
- postmortems

## What we deliberately do differently
Business discovery is adaptive:
- inspect outcomes early;
- update hypotheses;
- kill weak ideas quickly;
- conduct field tests;
- accept that the opportunity registry evolves.

## Evidence ladder
From weakest to strongest:
1. Interesting anecdote
2. Web evidence of similar businesses
3. Market / regulatory evidence
4. Supplier quote
5. Host / customer stated intent
6. Deposit / preorder / signed trial
7. Actual paid transaction
8. Repeat paid transaction
9. Positive unit economics over time
10. Replication across locations / customers

Scores must not hide where an idea sits on this ladder.

# Business Archetypes v1

This taxonomy is intentionally broad. Installation businesses are one family, not the project definition.

1. **Hosted micro-asset** — movable revenue-producing asset inside an existing customer flow
2. **Standalone micro-asset** — owner controls the asset and small footprint directly
3. **Rental asset** — one asset monetized repeatedly through time-based use
4. **Small-format space** — room, booth, studio, storage, work or entertainment space
5. **Route business** — multiple small sites serviced on a scheduled route
6. **Unattended retail / vending** — physical products sold with limited staff interaction
7. **Self-service equipment** — customer pays to use a machine or facility
8. **B2B equipment / consumables** — equipment or recurring supplies embedded in a workplace
9. **Recurring procurement / operator** — customer repeatedly purchases standardized goods through an operator
10. **Niche commerce** — differentiated product or distribution niche with controlled inventory
11. **Recommerce / refurbishment** — acquire, restore, resell or rent assets where operational edge matters
12. **Service operation** — productized real-world service whose labor can be scheduled, outsourced or automated
13. **Marketplace / brokerage with execution** — intermediary model only where it solves more than discovery / listing
14. **Small-scale production** — compact equipment converts inputs into a saleable output
15. **Physical-digital hybrid** — physical delivery / access / asset plus a digital operating layer
16. **Experience / entertainment** — compact paid experience with measurable utilization
17. **Infrastructure-like micro service** — charging, storage, access, dispensing, measurement or similar recurring utility
18. **Other** — keep the registry open to models not captured above

# Opportunity Scoring Framework v1

Use a 100-point score as a screening tool, not as truth.

## A. Economics — 25
- contribution margin potential — 5
- repeat / recurring revenue — 5
- plausible payback period — 5
- downside / residual value — 5
- cash conversion / working-capital burden — 5

## B. Owner Fit — 25
- predictable / batchable labor — 5
- low weekday interruption risk — 5
- low emergency response burden — 5
- owner-hours do not scale linearly with customers — 5
- tasks can be outsourced / automated — 5

## C. Demand & Distribution — 20
- clear existing customer pain / job-to-be-done — 5
- proven willingness to pay — 5
- reachable distribution / location access — 5
- substitute pressure is manageable — 5

## D. Asset / Operations — 15
- reversibility / mobility — 3
- resale or recoverable value — 3
- maintenance simplicity — 3
- supply / replenishment simplicity — 3
- installation / operating complexity — 3

## E. Competition & Defensibility — 10
- market not fully commoditized — 3
- local / operational edge can matter — 3
- data / relationships / location can compound — 2
- platform displacement risk manageable — 2

## F. AI / Data Leverage — 5
- AI can reduce owner labor or materially improve economics — 3
- operating data can accumulate into a useful proprietary asset — 2

## Hard gates
A candidate should normally not enter field validation when any is true:
- requires continuous weekday availability;
- depends on unverified legal permission central to the model;
- first-pilot loss exceeds an acceptable downside;
- no plausible route to real customer payment;
- general-purpose AI alone already solves almost all customer value;
- success requires a large fixed lease before demand validation.

## Evidence confidence
- C0: hypothesis only
- C1: desk research
- C2: supplier / competitor evidence
- C3: customer / host interviews
- C4: signed intent / deposit / trial
- C5: real transactions

# Korea Feasibility Source Notes v1

Observed 2026-10-03 unless noted otherwise.

These sources support the **structure** of the hard-gate screen. They are not sufficient by themselves to establish final profitability.

## Small-format / simulator capex

### GOLF24 unmanned screen-golf
https://parkgolf.playgts.com/content/intro_golf24

The operator currently presents a basic unmanned 15-pyeong configuration at roughly KRW 30m, with several construction/site items excluded. This supports excluding full simulator rooms from the first-pilot capital envelope.

## Small-scale production equipment

### Bambu Lab Korea authorized reseller pricing
https://dyairstar3d.com/3d-price-2026-08/

Published August 2026 pricing includes prosumer systems from below KRW 1m to several million won. This supports keeping small-scale 3D/personalization production inside the capital screen, subject to actual product demand.

## Luggage storage

### Seoul official tourism — Luggage Lounge Anguk Locker
https://korean.visitseoul.net/attractions/Luggage-Lounge-Anguk-Locker/KOPy2nopb

Seoul's official tourism site documents a dedicated unmanned luggage facility with multiple locker sizes, card kiosk and online reservation near Anguk/Bukchon. This validates the customer job while also demonstrating visible incumbent supply.

## Photo kiosk / hosted device models

### SELPIC business deployment
https://selpic.kr/en/business

The supplier describes multiple deployment structures including purchase, rental, event use and host-based installations. This supports testing host economics rather than assuming a standalone photo shop.

## Laundry

### Coinup
https://www.coin-up.co.kr/default/eng/business/business6.php

The Korean operator documents laundromat and shop-in-shop formats. The model is real, but utilities and heavy-equipment requirements remain a first-pilot concern.

## Utility printing

### Seoul tourism — Print Cafe
https://english.visitseoul.net/entertainment/printcafe/ENPz0djo8

This documents a 24-hour self-service print/copy/fax/scan concept in Seoul. It validates the use case but not the economics of a new standalone store.

## B2B procurement

### SERVEONE MRO
https://www.serveone.co.kr/kr/purchasing-service/mro.do

SERVEONE describes integrated purchasing for fragmented MRO categories. This supports recurring-procurement as a real value proposition but also shows strong incumbent capability, so a SideEconomy entry must be narrow and differentiated.

## Korean smart equipment ecosystem

### Small Business Smart Shop technology pool
https://www.sbiz.or.kr/smst/vars/con0302.html

The program lists deployable smart-store technology categories. This supports equipment availability and supplier depth but does not imply that any listed technology is independently profitable.

## Research caution

For every KEEP candidate, the next evidence stage should seek:
- direct Korean supplier price / rental quote;
- actual host economics;
- maintenance cadence;
- resale / exit route;
- direct competitor price;
- regulatory or insurance requirements.

Only then should a 100-point score be treated as decision-relevant.

# Transaction Validation Execution Pack v1

Use this file when moving from research to actual conversations.

## A. MRO buyer qualification

Target first:
- Seoul / Gyeonggi small manufacturing, fabrication, repair, logistics and workshop businesses;
- roughly 10-100 employees;
- recurring consumables;
- purchasing handled by owner, admin, plant manager or site supervisor rather than a dedicated strategic procurement team.

Public lead source:
- Korea Industrial Complex Corporation Factory On / Smart K-Factory states that its factory search is based on 200,000+ factory records.
- https://www.factoryon.go.kr/

Do not use employer customer lists or internal bank data.

### 10-minute discovery

1. 지난 한 달에 반복 구매한 안전·현장 소모품이 무엇이었나요?
2. 보통 몇 군데에서 주문하나요?
3. 주문은 누가 하나요?
4. 재고는 누가 확인하나요?
5. 최근 재고가 떨어져 급하게 산 사례가 있었나요?
6. 가격 비교에 시간을 얼마나 쓰나요?
7. 월 대략 얼마 정도 쓰나요? 범위만 가능.
8. 납품업체를 바꾸기 어려운 이유는 무엇인가요?
9. 10~20개 반복품목을 한 번에 비교견적하면 실제 검토해볼 수 있나요?
10. 현재 품목명/규격/최근 단가를 익명으로 5~10개 공유할 수 있나요?

### OC-021 extension questions

11. 장갑 같은 물품은 누가 직원에게 지급하나요?
12. 누가 얼마나 가져가는지 기록하나요?
13. 야간/주말에도 필요한가요?
14. 과다사용이나 분실이 실제 문제인가요?
15. 직원 인증 후 필요한 만큼만 꺼내는 지급기가 있다면 관리시간이 줄까요?
16. 기계가 무료/저가 설치되고 월 소모품 구매약정이 붙는 구조라면 검토 가능한가요?

## B. MRO quote worksheet

For every received basket record:
- exact product/spec;
- brand substitution allowed Y/N;
- monthly quantity;
- current unit price;
- current freight;
- current supplier;
- current lead time;
- urgent purchase frequency;
- proposed purchase cost;
- proposed customer price;
- fulfillment cost;
- payment cost;
- expected gross contribution;
- expected gross margin;
- owner minutes/order.

Do not call a basket viable solely because gross margin percentage is high. Low-ticket orders can still be owner-time negative.

## C. First MRO proposal structure

The proposal should contain only:
1. customer's existing basket;
2. like-for-like quote;
3. acceptable substitutions separately;
4. delivery cadence;
5. one consolidated settlement method;
6. reorder reminder / auto-replenishment method;
7. trial period;
8. no equipment.

Do not pitch AI.

## D. Event supplier call

Ask each supplier:
1. one-day net price;
2. partner/reseller pricing;
3. Seoul transport included?;
4. setup/removal included?;
5. staff required?;
6. consumable limit?;
7. custom frame/UI included?;
8. failure-response SLA?;
9. cancellation terms?;
10. minimum lead time?;
11. can our company/customer brand be front-facing?;
12. can the supplier invoice us rather than the end client?

Record all answers before buyer pricing.

## E. Event buyer discovery

Target role:
- HR/culture;
- marketing/brand;
- event agency PM;
- conference organizer;
- school/association event manager.

Questions:
1. 가장 최근 행사에서 포토존/참여형 콘텐츠에 얼마를 썼나요?
2. 단순 사진과 브랜드 체험 중 무엇이 목적이었나요?
3. 설치·철거를 별도로 조율하는 게 번거로웠나요?
4. 참가자 데이터/설문 연계가 필요했나요?
5. 다음 확정 행사는 언제인가요?
6. 날짜·장소·예상인원이 정해져 있다면 실제 견적을 받아보시겠어요?

Only a dated quote request counts as strong C3 evidence.

## F. Evidence coding

- C2: supplier/competitor quote or documented terms.
- C3-A: qualified interview only.
- C3-B: real basket or dated event requirements received.
- C3-C: actual quote requested.
- C4-A: written trial intent.
- C4-B: deposit / purchase order / paid order.
- C5: fulfilled transaction and observed economics.

## G. Stop rules

Stop MRO outreach after 15 qualified interviews if the C3 gate fails.

Stop event outreach after 20 qualified buyers if there is no real dated quote request.

Do not broaden the target segment mid-test without recording a protocol change.

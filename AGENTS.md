# AGENTS.md

## Mission
Help identify and validate business models that can create an independent side economy while the owner keeps a demanding full-time job.

## Non-negotiables
- Do not optimize for novelty or AI usage.
- Do not assume SaaS is superior to physical or hybrid businesses.
- Do not recommend a business merely because it is technically buildable.
- Prefer evidence of real customer spend, repeated demand, and executable distribution.
- Treat weekday interruption risk as a first-class cost.
- Distinguish sourced facts, estimates, and assumptions.
- Do not use employer-confidential information, employer customer data, or internal systems.
- Flag legal, licensing, tax, insurance, employment-policy, and conflict-of-interest questions before commercialization.
- No large capex commitment before field validation.
- Prefer reversible experiments.

## Research behavior
1. Record candidates in `research/opportunity-registry.csv`.
2. Use the common scoring framework; do not invent ad-hoc scores to favor a candidate.
3. Record source links and observation dates.
4. Keep assumptions explicit.
5. Before any pilot, write a preregistration with capex, expected economics, owner time, and PASS / MOVE / FAIL rules.
6. After pilot launch, do not silently change success criteria.
7. Add code only when it reduces repeated work or improves reproducibility.

## Default geography
Seoul / Seoul metropolitan area for early physical or hybrid pilots, unless a business model clearly benefits from a different scope.
